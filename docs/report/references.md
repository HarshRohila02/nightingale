## Working notes for the lead (delete before submission)

Proposals for `[citation needed]` markers in chapters this file's author does not own. None of these
files was edited.

  primary care, and one of the highest-stakes [citation needed: incidence of chest pain
  presentations]": entry 10 (Gulati et al. 2021), whose abstract says chest pain is a frequent cause of
  emergency department visits in the United States. It supports the emergency half, for the United
  States only; it does not support "primary care" or "highest-stakes", so either narrow the sentence
  or keep a marker for those.

  cannot reliably cite their sources, and are poorly calibrated": the file as read on 2026-11-12
  already cites "Ji et al. 2023" for "can state false facts", which is entry 28 (hallucination in text
  generation in general) and matches its record. Entry 30 (Pal et al. 2023; hallucination in the
  medical domain) could be added for the clinical setting. The marker for "cannot reliably cite their
  sources" and "poorly calibrated" has no verified reference and should stay.
- `01-introduction.md`, "that combination exists in the literature (`docs/00` §5) [citation needed:
  knowledge graphs combined with ML or LLMs for clinical decision support]": entries 29 (Jiang et al.
  2024; patient-specific graphs built from language models and biomedical graphs, used to train a
  neural network for healthcare predictions) and 31 (Rotmensch et al. 2017).
- `03-data.md`, "a first MI in the real world typically occurs in the patient's 60s [citation needed:
